# Delivery roadmap

1. **Foundation:** time boundaries, pick revisions, receipts, ledger scoring and schema.
2. **League play:** accounts, leagues, seasons, rounds, invitations and question authoring.
3. **Resolution:** moderator approvals, evidence, reveal policy, confidence tokens and disputes.
4. **Experience:** standings, achievements, watch progress, safe reactions and archives.
5. **Public readiness:** exports, moderation, accessibility, threat review, deployment and browser coverage.
